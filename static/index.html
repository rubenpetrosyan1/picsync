📥 How to export your Google Maps Timeline

  1. Go to Google Takeout
  2. Click "Deselect all" then scroll down and check "Location History"
  3. Click "Multiple formats" and choose JSON or KML (both supported)
  4. Click "OK" → "Next step"
  5. Choose "Export once" → "Create export"
  6. Wait for the email or download link, then unzip the archive
  7. Look for Timeline.json or Records.json (JSON format) or Timeline.kml (KML format)
  8. Click "Import Google Maps Timeline" above and select that file
